Script2Motion – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Script2Motion (“the app”) turns scripts into documentary videos on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

1. Content you create

Scripts, voice recordings, reference images, projects and exported videos are stored in the app’s private storage on your device, and videos are rendered and encoded there. The app has no account system, and the developer runs no server that receives your content. Parts of your text leave your device only in the cases described in sections 2 and 3.

2. Permissions and internet use

  • Microphone – used only while you record narration for a scene. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Auto voice – by default, scene narration is created by your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine (for example Google or Samsung), and the audio files stay on your device. If you choose a cloud voice instead, see section 3.
  • Save to gallery – used only when you tap “Save to gallery”, to add the exported video to your photo library. The app does not read your existing photos or videos.
  • Reference images – when you tap “Add images”, your phone’s system photo picker opens and only the pictures you choose are shared with the app. The app needs no photo permission and has no access to the rest of your gallery. A resized copy of each chosen picture is kept in the app’s private storage to make paper cut-outs, and it is never uploaded. You can remove it in the app at any time.
  • Internet – used to load and measure ads (see section 4), to check for app updates, to check that the phone is online before a video is created, and for the services below. These services can see your IP address, as with any web request, and handle it under their own policies.
    • NASA GIBS – satellite map images.
    • Pixabay – free photos and short video clips for scenes. Only a few keywords from the scene, such as “Dubai skyline”, are sent.
    • Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons – free-licensed photos of people, places and things named in your script. That name is sent. This is on by default, and you can turn it off with “Real photos from Wikipedia” when creating a video.

3. Optional AI features

AI features are off until you add your own API key in Settings → AI & voice. The app works without them. When you use them, your device connects directly to the provider you picked, and nothing passes through a server of ours. That provider handles your data under its own terms and privacy policy, so please read them.

  • Providers – Google Gemini; OpenAI or another OpenAI-compatible service you choose, such as OpenRouter, Groq or DeepSeek; and ElevenLabs for voices.
  • What is sent – your video title and script when writing or planning; each sentence of narration for a cloud voice; and a text description of the scene for AI pictures.
  • What is not sent – your voice recordings, reference images and exported videos.
  • Your keys – stored in the app’s private storage on your device and sent only to the provider they belong to.
  • AI pictures – off unless you turn them on, and never made of real people. Each one is labelled “AI-generated image” on screen.

4. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, app interactions and diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Data we collect

The developer does not collect, store or sell personal information, and does not receive your scripts, recordings, images, videos or API keys. Data reaches other companies only as described in sections 2, 3 and 4.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to family-friendly (PG) content.

7. Data retention and deletion

You can delete any video or project in the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device, including your API keys. App data is excluded from cloud backups. Text you sent to an AI provider is kept according to that provider’s policy, so contact the provider about deleting it.

8. Security

Content is kept in storage that other apps cannot access. The video engine runs in an isolated view that cannot open your files, and it can connect only to the map, photo and Wikimedia services listed in section 2.
